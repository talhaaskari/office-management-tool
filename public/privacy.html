Privacy Policy — Peace Staff

Peace Printing & Production Pvt. Ltd — Internal Staff Portal
Last updated: 24 November 2026

Peace Staff is an internal staff portal for the employees of Peace Printing & Production Pvt. Ltd. It is not a public service. This policy explains what information the portal collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Google sign-in information: when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo, as provided by your Google account.
  • Work information you create in the portal: tasks, task notes, daily reports, announcements and suggestions that you submit as part of your work.

How we use it

Your information is used only to operate the internal staff portal: to identify you, to manage your access (approved by an administrator), and to show your work to you and to your team administrators. We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with any third party for marketing or advertising.

Service providers

The portal is hosted by Vercel, its database is hosted by Supabase, and sign-in is provided by Google. These providers process data only as needed to run the service, under their own privacy policies.

Data retention and deletion

Your account and work data are kept while you are a member of staff. If you leave, or if you ask the portal administrator to remove your data, your account and personal information will be deleted from the portal.

Contact

For any question about this policy or your data, contact the portal administrator at peaceproduction93@gmail.com.
